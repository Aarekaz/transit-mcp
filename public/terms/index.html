Terms of Use

Transit MCP is a public, read-only informational service for current DC Metro and NYC Subway data. Use it as a convenient interface to transit feeds, not as an official guarantee.

Last updated August 21, 2026

Using the service

Transit MCP is an unofficial community project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by WMATA, MTA, OpenAI, Anthropic, or any transit agency. You may use it to retrieve public, read-only transit information through its website, server-information endpoint, and MCP tools. The service does not sell tickets, control transit operations, or modify agency data.

Live data has limits

WMATA and MTA control the availability and accuracy of their upstream feeds. Results can be delayed, incomplete, inaccurate, unavailable, omitted, stale, or changed by the agencies without notice. Transit MCP can also be interrupted by network, edge-platform, deployment, or maintenance events.

Do not rely on Transit MCP for an emergency or other safety-critical decision. Do not treat any result as a guaranteed arrival. Use official agency alerts and emergency services when timing or safety is critical.

Fair use and rate limits

Please use reasonable request rates. Abuse, disruption, attempts to circumvent service protections, and unlawful use are prohibited. Avoid automated traffic that disrupts the service or its upstream providers. Transit MCP uses abuse controls and rate limits, currently configured at roughly 300 requests per 60-second window per source-IP key per Cloudflare location. Shared networks can share that allowance, and enforcement is eventually consistent.

Requests may be refused, throttled, or limited to protect the service, other users, and upstream systems. The policy can change as operational needs change.
